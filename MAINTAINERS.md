# Maintainers

Maintainers are the small, trusted core team that reviews and merges pull requests, stewards the
content model, and shepherds the project's direction. This is an **unofficial, community-built**
project — not a Microsoft team.

that team.

## What maintainers do

- Review and merge pull requests against `main`.
- Triage issues — label, prioritize, and close stale or out-of-scope ones.
- Steward the content model ([`CONTENT-MODEL.md`](CONTENT-MODEL.md)) and the style bar
  ([`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Keep CI green and dependencies fresh.
- Enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and respond to private reports.
- Make small decisions in the open on issues and PRs; raise larger direction changes (new top-level
  sections, scope, name, license) in a dedicated issue first.

## How decisions are made

Rough consensus among active maintainers, in public on issues and PRs. If maintainers disagree, the
maintainer who owns the affected area (per CODEOWNERS) gets the deciding voice; if that's still
unclear, we wait for a third maintainer's input.

## How to become a maintainer

There's no formal application process. The path is:

1. Contribute consistently and well — multiple merged PRs across walkthroughs, fixes, or tooling.
2. Help with reviews and triage on others' PRs and issues.
3. An existing maintainer nominates you in an issue; if the rest of the maintainers agree (no
   objections within a week), you're invited to the team.

What we look for: judgment about scope and tone, respect for the content model, the unofficial framing,
and the screenshot-honesty rule, and the ability to disagree gracefully in public.

## Stepping back

Maintaining is volunteer work. Maintainers can step back at any time — open an issue or message the
team, and we'll move you to "emeritus" in this file. Coming back later is welcome.
